    Noise Beats Coarseness: The Value of Inside Information in the Kyle-Back Model

    Two signals that explain the same share of a normal fundamental's variance can be worth very different amounts to an insider in the continuous-time Kyle model. The sign of the fundamental explains 63.7 percent of its variance and earns 63.7 percent of the full-information profit, while normal noisy information of the same precision earns 79.8 percent. The insider must steer order flow to a normally distributed endpoint to stay hidden, so information whose shape is far from normal is worth less.
