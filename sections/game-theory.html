    Who Bargains Next? Recognition and Cooperative Allocations

    Who proposes after an offer is rejected looks like a procedural detail, but it changes the outcome of coalitional bargaining. If the player who rejects proposes next, patient players no longer reach the Shapley value: every player's deviation from an equal share is amplified.

    The Information Complexity of Nash Equilibrium

    How many noisy observations of payoffs are needed to find an approximate Nash equilibrium of a small zero-sum game with high confidence? The answer depends only on the equilibrium mixing probabilities, is largest when equilibrium play is uniform, and can be far smaller than estimating every payoff first and then solving the game.
