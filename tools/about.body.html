About

Equilibrium Daily publishes one short note a day. Each note states one result in economics or finance, proves it, and comes with a PDF.

How notes are produced

  1. A person sets the task: the result to be stated and proved.
  2. An AI model drafts the note and checks the proof.
  3. A person reviews the note before publication.

Accuracy

Results and proofs are checked with current advanced large language models. The accuracy of each note therefore depends on these models: a note can contain errors even after this check and human review. Readers can judge each proof for themselves from the PDF.

Corrections

We reserve the right to correct or modify any note, including its result, after publication. To point out a possible error, write to us on X at @equilibriumdail; we decide whether and how to correct the note.

Use of the notes

You may use, share and adapt the notes as you wish. Equilibrium Daily publishes results openly, without waiting for the traditional journal review process: we think knowledge should be available to everyone rather than held back by gatekeeping. Why we publish this way.
